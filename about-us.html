The Story Behind.

Inspiration comes in many different forms. Mine came about when interacting with Engineers basically Civil Engineers and Quantitity Surveyors.

Having interacted with a Quantity Surveyor ( A fancy name for a person who calculates what is needed for a building to be built to completion ) On construction sites, the major hurdle is stock taking of what remains and what lags behind, because a slight delay may cause the trickle effect into the whole plan. Thus, having a good number of materials on site ensures the continuity of events.

Thus, the idea of a construction stock taking was born.
